Import from the watch
Easiest: screenshots. In the Fitbit app, open the kid's weekly Movement list and screenshot it — one screenshot per week, several at a time is fine. The pictures are read right here on this device, you get to check the numbers before they are saved, and nothing is uploaded anywhere. (Lo mas facil: capturas de pantalla de la lista Movement semanal. Se leen aqui mismo y revisas los numeros antes de guardar.)
For a big backfill, a Google Takeout export also works: tick only Fitbit, download the .zip and drop it in whole. Loose .json/.csv steps files work too. (Para el historial completo tambien sirve el .zip de Google Takeout.)
No export? Just read the number off the watch face and type it in above. One tap a day is enough to see the week fill up. (Sin exportacion: lee el numero en el reloj y escribelo arriba.)
