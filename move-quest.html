⌚ Today from the watch
Easiest: screenshots. In the Fitbit app, open the kid's weekly Movement list and screenshot it — several at a time is fine, and steps and active/light minutes are read together. The pictures are read on this device, you check the numbers before they are saved, and nothing is uploaded anywhere. A Google Takeout .zip or loose .json/.csv files also work for big backfills. (Lo mas facil: capturas de la lista Movement — se leen pasos y minutos aqui mismo, y revisas antes de guardar. Tambien sirve el .zip de Google Takeout.)
