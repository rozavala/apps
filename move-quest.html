Import from a watch export
In Google Takeout, tick only Fitbit and download the .zip. Drop the whole zip in here — every month of steps inside it is read at once, and if the export covers more than one child you get to pick whose folder to use. Loose .json or .csv files work too; you can select several at a time. Everything is read on this device and nothing is uploaded anywhere. (En Google Takeout marca solo Fitbit y descarga el .zip: se lee entero, aqui mismo, sin subir nada a ninguna parte.)
No export? Just read the number off the watch face and type it in above. One tap a day is enough to see the week fill up. (Sin exportacion: lee el numero en el reloj y escribelo arriba.)
